# Glossary

**Social account**: a place Oreolis posts, with its link, a commissioned artwork and that artwork's credit. One record in `socials` in `src/content.js`.

**Main social**: a social account with `main: true`. It appears in the hero menu and under MAIN SOCIALS; every other account appears under OTHER SOCIALS.

**Artwork credit**: the artist's handle shown on a social account's card as "Art by: © handle".
